    The weekend waitlist

    No line on the sidewalk

    We do not take reservations on Saturdays and Sundays. Instead, everyone goes on one list, in order, and nobody has to stand in the cold.

    1. Add your name from anywhereUse the waitlist on our home page from your sofa, or give your name at the door.
    2. We text you ten minutes beforeWalk over to Jimmie Simpson Park or grab a coffee to go from the window. Your phone tells you when to head back.
    3. Reply if you are running lateWe hold the table for ten minutes after the text. Reply “late” and we move you down one place instead of off the list.
    4. Sit down when everyone is hereWe seat the whole party at once, so the table does not sit half empty while the line grows.

    Good to know

    Plan around what you need

    High chairs and a kids' plate

    Six high chairs and two booster seats. Under-10s get half pancakes or a small benny for $9, and crayons come with the cutlery.

    Room for a stroller

    Park it by the window bench and we keep the aisle clear. On a busy Sunday, folded is kinder to everyone.

    Accessible washroom

    On the main floor, with grab bars, room to turn a wheelchair, and the change table.

    Step-free from the sidewalk

    A level entrance on Queen Street East and a wide aisle all the way to the back tables.

    A quieter table

    Ask for the back corner, away from the coffee grinder. Weekday mornings before ten are the calmest time to come.

    Dogs on the patio

    Open May to October, weather allowing. Water bowls by the gate and a hook for the leash. Inside, service dogs only.

    Parties of up to six

    Add everyone to the waitlist as one party. We seat you as soon as the whole group has arrived.

    Seven or more

    On weekends we cannot promise a table that big. On Tuesdays the whole room can be yours: see private events.

    Vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free

    Every dish is tagged on the menu. Swap the muffin for a potato rösti and your benny has no gluten in the base.

    Allergies

    Tell your server before you order. The kitchen is small, so we will be honest about what we can and cannot promise.

    Short answers

    Can I book a table?

    Not on weekends: the waitlist keeps it fair for everyone. On weekdays there is rarely more than a ten-minute wait, so just walk in.

    When can I join the waitlist?

    From 7:30 on weekend mornings. The first names on the list sit down at 8:00.

    Do you do takeout?

    Coffee and juice to go from the window, all day. The plates are made for the table, so they stay here.

    Can I work on my laptop?

    Weekdays, yes, and there is Wi-Fi. On weekends we ask laptops to wait until after 1pm, when the line has gone.
