Gift cards

A proper haircut,
in an envelope.

£25 and £50 cards, sold at the desk and sealed in an envelope that looks like an old cinema ticket. Good for any cut, beard or shave, with any barber, for twelve months.

Make one up

See it before you buy it

Pick an amount and the names. We will have it sealed and waiting at the desk, and you pay when you collect.

    At the desk

    Two minutes, start to finish

    1. Call in, Monday to SaturdayWhoever is not mid-cut will sort it. No need to wait in the queue for a chair.
    2. Pay by card or cashWe write the name and number on the stub, and the same number in the book behind the till.
    3. Hand it overSealed in a red envelope with a ticket window. They bring it in, or just give their name.

    Using it

    The small print, in big letters

    Valid for
    Twelve months from the date on the stub.
    Barbers
    Dev, Marcus or Joe, walk-in or booked.
    Covers
    Anything on the price list, kids' cuts included.
    Change
    If the cut costs less, we write what is left on the back.
    Lost it?
    Tell us the name. The number is in the book.

    Questions at the desk

    Can I buy one online?

    No, only at the desk. Fill in the form above and we have it sealed and waiting, so it takes a minute when you come in.

    Can I put more than £50 on one card?

    Buy two. Two envelopes look better than one anyway.

    Can the leftover go on a tip?

    Yes. Tell your barber to keep the change and we note it in the book. Tips go straight to them, as always.

    Does it work on quiet Tuesday mornings?

    Yes. From 9 to 11 on Tuesdays the radio is off and the lights are low. A good first visit for someone who finds barbers loud.

    Can I swap it for cash?

    No, sorry. But it never stops being a haircut while it is valid.
