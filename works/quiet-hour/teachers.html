    About the teachers

    Can I book a private class?

    Yes, with any teacher: $95 for an hour in the studio, for one or two people. Ask at the desk or reply to any booking email.

    What if my teacher is away?

    Cover teachers are on the timetable by the evening before. If you only come for one person, cancel for free up to two hours before class.

    How are teachers trained?

    Everyone has at least 500 hours of teacher training and a current first aid certificate, and the five of them practise together every Monday afternoon.
