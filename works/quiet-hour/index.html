    Questions people ask before a first class

    I'm not flexible. Is that a problem?

    Not at all. Most people start stiff. Teachers offer an easier version of every pose, and blocks bring the floor closer.

    What should I wear?

    Anything you can move in. We practise barefoot. There is a changing room and a place for your bag.

    Can I cancel a booking?

    Yes, up to two hours before class, straight from the confirmation email. Later than that, the class is used.

    How do I get there?

    The 501 Queen streetcar stops outside. There is bike parking at the door and street parking on the side streets.
