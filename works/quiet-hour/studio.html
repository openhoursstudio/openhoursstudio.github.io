The studio

A small room,
made for every body.

Fourteen mats above a bakery on Queen Street East. Warm floors in winter, windows open in summer, and enough props for everyone.

How we run it

Four promises

Pay what you can, on Sundays

The 11:30 Restore class is $5 to $25, you choose. No questions, no proof.

Props for every pose

Blocks, straps, bolsters and chairs. Using them is not cheating, it is how teachers practise too.

Step-free and quiet

A lift from the street, a gender-neutral changing room and no music in most classes.

Clean between classes

Mats and props are wiped after every class, blankets washed every day.

About the space

Are there showers?

No, but there are two changing rooms with mirrors, hooks and a hairdryer.

Can I bring my own mat?

Of course. Ours are free to use and cleaned after every class, if you prefer not to carry one.

Can I rent the room?

On weekday afternoons, yes: for small workshops, photo shoots or a quiet meeting. Ask at the desk.
