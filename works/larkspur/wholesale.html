How it starts

Taste first, then decide

  1. A tasting box, on usTwo loaves and a dozen pastries, dropped on a Tuesday morning. Put them out and see what sells.
  2. A standing orderThe same order every week, so we can plan the dough. Change it by the cut-off whenever you need to.
  3. A check-in after two weeksWe do not take back unsold bread, so after a fortnight we look at what is left over and adjust the numbers with you.
